This document describes, in plain language, who can apply for each State
Bank of India (SBI) credit card referenced by this system. It is intended
purely as searchable retrieval content for the recommendation assistant and
is written independently of the internal eligibility spreadsheet used for
ground truth.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Income Requirement
Applicants interested in the SimplyCLICK card should be earning a gross
annual income of at least six lakh rupees (₹6,00,000) if they are salaried
professionals. Freelancers and self-employed applicants are not currently
accepted for this particular card.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Age Requirement
The primary cardholder must be at least 21 years of age at the time of
application. There is no stated upper age limit for salaried applicants of
this card.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Credit History Requirement
An applicant's bureau score should generally sit at 750 or higher,
reflecting a healthy, well-managed repayment history over the preceding two
to three years.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Employment and Relationship
Only salaried employees of recognised private or public sector companies
and government organisations are eligible. SBI does not insist that the
applicant already hold a savings or salary account with the bank before
applying for SimplyCLICK.

### SBI SimplySAVE – Income Requirement
This entry-level card is more accessible: applicants of any employment
type, whether drawing a monthly salary or running their own business, need
a minimum yearly income of roughly three lakh rupees (₹3,00,000).

### SBI SimplySAVE – Age and Credit Score
Applicants should be adults of at least 21 years, and a CIBIL score around
700 or above is typically expected to be considered creditworthy for this
product.

### SBI SimplySAVE – Employment and Relationship
Because this is designed as a mass-market card, SBI accepts salaried
employees, self-employed professionals, and small business owners alike,
and no prior banking relationship with SBI is required.

### SBI Prime – Income Requirement
SBI Prime is a premium travel and lifestyle card reserved for higher
earners. A minimum annual income of fifteen lakh rupees (₹15,00,000) is
expected from the applicant.

### SBI Prime – Age, Credit Score and Employment
The cardholder should be at least 21 years old, hold a strong credit
bureau score of 750 or higher, and be either salaried employee or a
self-employed professional with demonstrable, stable income.

### SBI Prime – Relationship Requirement
No pre-existing SBI account is mandated for Prime applicants, although
having one may speed up document verification.

### SBI Elite – Income Requirement
SBI Elite sits at the top of SBI's consumer card range and is intended for
affluent customers with an annual income of twenty lakh rupees
(₹20,00,000) or more.

### SBI Elite – Age, Credit Score and Employment
Applicants must be adults of at least 21 years with an excellent bureau
score, typically 780 and above, and must be salaried, self-employed, or an
established business owner.

### SBI Elite – Relationship Requirement
Unlike SBI's other cards described here, Elite applicants are expected to
already maintain an active savings, salary, or current account
relationship with State Bank of India before they apply.
