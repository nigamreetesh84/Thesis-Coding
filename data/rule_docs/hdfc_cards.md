# HDFC Bank – Credit Card Eligibility Rules

The notes below summarise, in everyday language, the eligibility
expectations for the HDFC Bank credit cards supported by this assistant.
They exist solely as searchable reference material for the retrieval
layer and are worded independently of the internal eligibility
spreadsheet used for ground truth.

### HDFC MoneyBack+ – Income Requirement
MoneyBack+ is HDFC's entry-level cashback card. Applicants need a modest
annual income of about two-and-a-half lakh rupees (₹2,50,000), regardless
of their employment category.

### HDFC MoneyBack+ – Age and Credit Score
Applicants as young as 18 can apply, provided they can show some
independent income, and a CIBIL score of 650 or above is generally
sufficient.

### HDFC MoneyBack+ – Employment and Relationship
Salaried staff, self-employed professionals, and small business owners are
all welcome to apply, and there is no requirement to already have a
HDFC.

### HDFC Millennia – Income Requirement
Millennia targets young professionals and asks for a minimum gross annual
income of three-and-a-half lakh rupees (₹3,50,000).

### HDFC Millennia – Age, Credit Score and Employment
The applicant should be at least 21 years old with a credit score around
700 or better, and should be either in salaried employment or
self-employed.

### HDFC Millennia – Relationship Requirement
No prior HDFC account is necessary in order to apply for Millennia.

### HDFC Regalia – Income Requirement
Regalia is positioned as a premium travel rewards card and expects an
annual income of at least twelve lakh rupees (₹12,00,000).

### HDFC Regalia – Age, Credit Score and Employment
Applicants must be 21 or older, carry a strong bureau score of roughly 750
or above, and be salaried or self-employed with verifiable earnings.

### HDFC Regalia – Relationship Requirement
An existing HDFC banking relationship is not compulsory for Regalia
applicants.

### HDFC Diners Club Black – Income Requirement
Diners Club Black is HDFC's flagship super-premium card, reserved for high
earners with an annual income of twenty-five lakh rupees (₹25,00,000) or
more.

### HDFC Diners Club Black – Age, Credit Score and Employment
The primary applicant should be at least 21 years old, maintain an
excellent credit score of 800 or better, and be either a salaried senior
professional or an established business owner.

### HDFC Diners Club Black – Relationship Requirement
Given its exclusivity, HDFC expects Diners Club Black applicants to
already hold an active account relationship with the bank prior to
applying.
