State Bank of India card eligibility, as summarised from customer-service
notes. Figures were revised in April 2026; where an older figure is quoted
it is for reference only and no longer applies.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Income Requirement
SimplyCLICK is offered to salaried people taking home a gross pay of at
least ₹50,000 every month. The card carries an annual fee of ₹499, which is
waived once yearly spends cross ₹1,00,000; neither figure affects who may
apply.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Age Requirement
Applications from anyone younger than twenty-one are declined. There is no
upper age limit.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Credit History Requirement
A bureau score under 750 leads to rejection, so applicants should be at
750 or above.

### SBI SimplyCLICK – Employment and Relationship
Self-employed professionals and business owners cannot apply for this card;
it is open to salaried employees only. Applicants do not need to bank with
SBI already.

### SBI SimplySAVE – Income Requirement
SimplySAVE asks for yearly earnings of no less than three lakh rupees,
whatever the source of that income. (Before April 2026 the floor was two
lakh rupees; that older figure no longer applies.)

### SBI SimplySAVE – Age and Credit Score
Applicants must have turned twenty-one. Credit scores of seven hundred and
above are accepted; lower scores are not.

### SBI SimplySAVE – Employment and Relationship
Salaried staff, the self-employed and business owners are all eligible, and
an existing SBI account is not needed.

### SBI Prime – Income Requirement
Prime was available from twelve lakh rupees a year until March 2026. From
April 2026 the minimum is ₹15,00,000 of annual income, and only the new
figure applies.

### SBI Prime – Age, Credit Score and Employment
Prime holders must be at least 21 and carry a CIBIL score of 750 or more
(the SimplySAVE threshold of 700 does not apply here). Salaried and
self-employed applicants qualify; business owners do not.

### SBI Prime – Relationship Requirement
Holding an SBI account beforehand is optional for Prime.

### SBI Elite – Income Requirement
Elite needs an annual income of twenty lakh rupees or higher. Spending of
₹10,00,000 a year earns a fee reversal, which is a benefit, not an
eligibility condition.

### SBI Elite – Age, Credit Score and Employment
Applicants must be at least 21. Prime needs a score of 750, but Elite
requires 780 or above. Salaried, self-employed and business-owner
applicants are all considered.

### SBI Elite – Relationship Requirement
Elite is for existing SBI customers only: applicants without an active SBI
savings, salary or current account are not considered.
