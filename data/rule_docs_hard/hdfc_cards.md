HDFC Bank card eligibility, as summarised from branch guidance. Amounts
are in Indian rupees; one lakh is 1,00,000.

### HDFC MoneyBack+ – Income Requirement
MoneyBack+ needs an income of two and a half lakh rupees a year, from any
kind of work. Cashback is capped at ₹2,000 a month; that cap is a benefit
limit, not an eligibility condition.

### HDFC MoneyBack+ – Age and Credit Score
The youngest applicants accepted are eighteen. A CIBIL score below six
hundred and fifty means the application will not go ahead.

### HDFC MoneyBack+ – Employment and Relationship
Every employment type is welcome, whether salaried, self-employed or
running a business. No HDFC account is required.

### HDFC Millennia – Income Requirement
Millennia requires at least three and a half lakh rupees a year. (Regalia,
by contrast, needs twelve lakh; that figure does not apply to Millennia.)

### HDFC Millennia – Age, Credit Score and Employment
Applicants under 21 are not accepted, and a score of 700 or higher is
needed. It is open to salaried and self-employed people but not to
business owners.

### HDFC Millennia – Relationship Requirement
You do not have to be an HDFC customer to apply.

### HDFC Regalia – Income Requirement
Regalia applicants should earn one lakh rupees a month or more, that is,
twelve lakh a year. The ₹2,500 joining fee is refunded after ₹1,00,000 of
spending in the first ninety days.

### HDFC Regalia – Age, Credit Score and Employment
Applicants must be twenty-one or older with a credit score of at least
seven hundred and fifty. Salaried and self-employed people are accepted;
business owners are not.

### HDFC Regalia – Relationship Requirement
An existing HDFC relationship is helpful but not compulsory.

### HDFC Diners Club Black – Income Requirement
Diners Club Black is reserved for incomes of twenty-five lakh rupees a
year and above. (Until 2025 the bar was ₹21,00,000; it has since been
raised.)

### HDFC Diners Club Black – Age, Credit Score and Employment
Only applicants aged 21 or more with a credit score of eight hundred or
better are considered. Salaried senior professionals and established
business owners qualify; self-employed professionals do not.

### HDFC Diners Club Black – Relationship Requirement
Only existing HDFC customers may apply; anyone without an active HDFC
account will be turned away.
